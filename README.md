
# Derovest

**Derovest** is a technology company focused on building practical, customizable software products that help businesses and organizations manage their everyday operations more efficiently.

We believe software should be **simple to use, flexible to configure, and affordable to grow with**.

## What is Derovest?

Derovest builds and develops software products for organizations that need better ways to manage their operations, people, finances, and day-to-day workflows.

Our approach is product-focused: identify real operational problems, build practical solutions, and continuously improve them based on real-world requirements.

## Our Products

### Mentivax

**Mentivax** is a customizable school management and school ERP platform developed by Derovest.

It is designed to help schools manage their core administrative and operational activities from a centralized system.

Key areas include:

* Student management
* Staff management
* Student attendance
* Staff attendance
* Staff leave management
* Staff payroll
* Fees and collections
* Expense management
* Reports and dashboards
* School configuration and setup

Mentivax is designed with customization in mind, allowing schools to adapt the system to their own workflows and requirements.

**Mentivax website:** https://www.mentivax.com
